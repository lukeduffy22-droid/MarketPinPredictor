# MarketPin heartbeat operating protocol

This is the durable, versioned operating protocol for the `market-open-readiness-watch` heartbeat.
The automation dispatcher must read this file completely before every substantive scan.
This file is instructions for that scoped heartbeat only; it never authorizes trade execution, formula promotion, broad process recovery, credential exposure, or substitution of stale/fallback data.

## Opening timing amendment

At the 08:30 and 09:30 CT heartbeat, wait until :15 seconds past the minute before invoking the compact opening-capture inspector. This makes the exact opening bucket and final 60-minute sample observable without creating a five-minute blind spot. This timing amendment is authoritative where the inherited protocol below is less explicit.

Continue the MarketPinPredictor directional-shift watch in C:\Cprojectsgpu_app\MarketPinPredictor. Use $marketpin-live-session-operator and the repository's canonical .venv Python. This heartbeat is observation-only except for its narrowly scoped monitoring journal and state writes. `tools\prepare_market_monitor_session.py` is the sole writer for session rollover, `tools\commit_market_monitor_scan.py` is the sole writer for each substantive scan, its deterministic helper wrappers, policy state, and cadence state, `tools\ack_market_monitor_notifications.py` is the sole writer that may acknowledge proven-delivered policy notifications, `tools\commit_market_monitor_opening_acceptance.py` is the sole writer for opening-acceptance milestones, `tools\ack_market_monitor_opening_acceptance.py` is the sole writer that may acknowledge proven-delivered opening-milestone notifications, `tools\commit_market_monitor_universe_prestage.py` is the sole writer for the 07:00 universe pre-stage receipt/outbox, and `tools\ack_market_monitor_universe_prestage.py` is the sole writer that may acknowledge a proven-delivered universe pre-stage alert. Never bypass those tools with a direct journal append or state edit for records they own. Never stop, restart, relaunch, or kill a process; never alter formulas, weights, subscriptions, universe selection, replay settings, application databases, or live trading behavior; and never open another Databento connection. The existing MarketPin scheduled launcher/watchdog remains the sole recovery owner. Diagnose failures and alert with evidence instead of attempting recovery.

Use America/Chicago time. On weekdays from 07:00 through 07:40 CT, perform the read-only clock preflight on the 00, 15, and 30 minute marks: inspect W32Time status and take a short w32tm stripchart sample without opening Databento. At 07:15 and 07:30 on a calendar-verified open session, also perform the bounded provider-free universe pre-stage postcondition check defined below; it reads only Task Scheduler history and the existing watchdog log and must not query HTTP, Databento, or an application database. Stay quiet when the clock is synchronized with absolute offset no more than 0.25 seconds. If Leap Indicator is 3 or the median absolute offset exceeds 0.25 seconds, notify once with the measured offset and report that the installed elevated 07:00/07:45 clock preflight failed to establish synchronization. The registered startup task now performs a synchronous `w32tm /resync /rediscover` and bounded status check; remain observation-only and persist/deduplicate the clock alert in monitor state. Outside a verified subscription window, including post-close, weekends, and holidays, `websocket=off_hours`, `subscription_suppressed=true`, and no new subscription generation are expected healthy lifecycle evidence; do not alert merely because live prediction readiness is false then. From 07:45 through 15:20 CT on an open session, operate the market monitor as follows. A weekday clock does not prove the exchange is open; distinguish a verified holiday or exceptional session from a feed failure. The scheduler wakes every five minutes solely to support adaptive cadence. A pending universe pre-stage alert, nonempty `monitor_scan_ledger.pending_notification_event_ids`, or nonempty `opening_acceptance.pending_notification_event_ids` makes every scheduled wake due for outbox service. Service the independent universe pre-stage outbox first, then the policy and opening outboxes, before substantive-scan cadence gating or session rollover; none requires a new market-data scan. The pre-stage journal is deliberately independent of `state.json`: its pending alert must neither block nor be erased by main session rollover, startup, gamma capture, or ORB capture. Treat an absent opening pending list as empty only for the legacy reset shape until its owner creates the current-session field. If policy and opening outboxes are both pending, service them serially through their separate acknowledgement owners, re-read state after each accepted acknowledgement, and use a fresh strictly increasing actual UTC acknowledgement time for the next owner. In NORMAL mode, perform a substantive scan only on the 00, 15, 30, and 45 minute marks, or when the last durably committed substantive scan (`state.json.last_scan_utc`) is at least 15 minutes old. In ELEVATED mode, perform a substantive scan on every five-minute wake. On an early, late, or non-due NORMAL wake, do not post a user-facing message.

Alert rules:

MAX PAIN — Alert on every newly confirmed max-pain value different from the last alerted value for that symbol. Confirm across two eligible observations with the same subscription_epoch_id, subscription generation, primary expiration, formula version, universe hash, and nonfallback full-OI source. Report old/new values, point and percent change, spot relationship, source/as-of timestamps, and corroboration. If expiration, universe, formula, or source changed, still report the changed displayed value but label it a provenance reset rather than a directional shift. Never infer direction from max pain alone and never repeat an unchanged value.

GAMMA PIN — A significant shift is an absolute pin change from the preceding eligible 15-minute baseline or last alerted pin of at least max(0.15% of spot, two times the median observed top-strike spacing). If spacing/history is insufficient, use conservative fallbacks of 20 SPX points and 75 NDX points. A pin migration across spot or zero gamma is also significant. Confirm on two consecutive eligible scans. Suppress a leader-flip alert while pin_is_contested is true or the leader's advantage is no more than 10%, unless spot also crosses the level; re-enable when the new leader is confirmed above that contest threshold. Report the exact threshold and contest evidence used.

DIRECTIONAL SHIFT — Alert only when two consecutive eligible scans contain at least two independent confirmations aligned in the same direction, with at least one price/forecast-regime confirmation and one options-structure or adequately classified research-flow confirmation. Price/forecast evidence may be a persistent spot cross of gamma pin or zero gamma, a completed provenance-aligned 5- or 15-minute opening-range breakout that persists for two eligible scans, a 15-minute spot move of at least 0.20%, or a forecast bias flip with abs(expected_move_pct) at least 0.10% or its interval entirely above/below spot. Options evidence may be a confirmed significant pin shift, a normalized net_gex/gross_gex sign change with magnitude at least 0.10 on both sides or a change of at least 0.25, a confirmed zero-gamma/GEX-wall regime cross, or a fresh research-only tape score crossing +0.15/-0.15 with adequate classified share. Independent SPX/NDX agreement strengthens but is not required. Max pain, aggregate OI, and inside-spread flow are supporting context only. Surface contradictions and abstain when confluence is insufficient.

DATA QUALITY — Alert on a new regular-hours loss of freshness, advancement, current generation, validation/exclusion eligibility, nonfallback provenance, GEX identity, WAL/quick-check integrity, or required-table persistence, and on material reconnect/queue/skipped-record deterioration. Do not let VIX-only thin-chain invalidity downgrade otherwise eligible SPX/NDX.

The scan-commit tool is the sole cadence authority. Under the same lock and in the same receipt-first transaction, it enters or extends ELEVATED mode for at least 30 minutes after any generated policy alert or any asserted adaptive trigger: a half-threshold pin move; contested pin leadership; spot within 0.10% of or crossing gamma pin, zero gamma, or a reported GEX wall; normalized net GEX approaching or crossing zero; a forecast bias awaiting confirmation; a HIGH volatility regime; or a new reconnect/stale/invalid/persistence event. A trigger while already ELEVATED restarts the 30-minute minimum from that scan when this extends the existing deadline and resets the stable count. After the minimum, only a fully eligible, trigger-free scan arriving 4–7 minutes after the preceding substantive scan can increment the stable count; an active data-quality condition, ineligible symbol, trigger, or missed wake resets it. The third qualifying scan returns to NORMAL. The commit receipt records the full replayable cadence decision and pre/next cadence hashes, while the atomic state replacement applies the next mode.

Only surface pending policy alerts, material recovery evidence after a data-quality alert, and one concise post-close learning recap after 15:05 CT. Cadence transitions are silent scheduling state, not user alerts. `event_ids_pending_notification`—not `event_ids_appended`, `event_ids_recovered`, the action name, or receipt fields—is the sole policy-notification outbox authority. After any safe successful scan result, re-read every still-pending wrapper from the originating current-session journal, verify its full record against the committed receipt, and construct one concise notification from those durable wrappers in pending order.
