# Post-close OPRA research collection

Implemented September 16, 2026; live activation and observed post-close provider
coverage are not yet verified.

The main streamer's connection window now ends 15 minutes after the reviewed
cash close: 15:15 America/Chicago on normal days, 12:15 on configured early-close
days. Weekends and holidays remain excluded. This is the options closing tail,
not a claim of curb-session or overnight OPRA coverage. Existing contract and
subscription staging limits remain intact; no additional provider client starts.

`subscription_window` now exposes separate `cash_close_utc` and
`collection_close_utc` timestamps, `prediction_session_allowed`, and the
`post_close_research` state. The cash-close timestamp is unchanged.

While the cash market is closed, the compute worker samples accepted quotes
at most once every five seconds and appends to
`logs/post_close_quotes/YYYY-MM-DD.ndjson`. Sampling is outside the ingest loop.
Quotes must have the captured generation, event/receive times within the
post-close window, no future timestamp, and receive age at most 30 seconds.
The file records excluded counts and empty samples as well as eligible samples.
It is not a lossless quote/trade tape, and does not assert product-specific
contract trading eligibility. A quote observed before the close is not relabelled
as post-close evidence merely because it remains cached.

Rows carry process epoch, generation, session timestamps and explicit
`diagnostic_research_only` / `usable_for_prediction=false` fields. No ORB,
forecast, gamma calculation or regular-session close result is generated by
this path. Historical files are appended, never rewritten. The separate
closing-tape recorder already configures cash-close plus 20 minutes for capture
and graceful shutdown; its observed completeness must be checked independently.

Validation: 148 tests passed across `tests/test_databento_streamer.py` and
`tests/test_post_close_quotes.py`, including regular/early-close boundaries,
holiday suppression, append preservation, generation/timestamp exclusions and
no post-close production calculation.

Activation requires loading the new backend source through the guarded launcher,
preserving `-EnableRutCanary` for the currently configured deployment. Check the
loaded fingerprint, then observe a session crossing the cash close: advancing
provider timestamps in the new file, stable epoch, no overload increase, unchanged
cash predictions and connection suppression at collection close. A restart or
passing tests is not evidence that the provider delivered post-close quotes.
